# Software

This folder contains the software and simulation-related work used for analysing the passive cooling house.

## CFD Analysis

The airflow and thermal behaviour of the house were studied using **ANSYS Workbench CFD**.

### Analysis Workflow

1. **Geometry Preparation**
   The passive cooling house geometry was prepared and imported into ANSYS Workbench.

2. **Fluid Domain**
   Air was defined as an incompressible fluid for analysing airflow inside the house.

3. **Boundary Conditions**

   * Velocity inlet based on local wind data
   * Pressure outlet at the exhaust opening
   * Heat flux or temperature conditions for walls and roof

4. **CFD Simulation**
   The simulation was performed to study airflow patterns, velocity distribution, and temperature distribution.

5. **Result Analysis**
   Airflow circulation, thermal stratification, temperature distribution, and cooling effectiveness were analysed.

6. **Experimental Validation**
   CFD results were compared with temperature measurements obtained from the 60 cm × 60 cm prototype.

## Purpose

The CFD analysis was used to understand how the passive cooling design improves natural airflow and reduces indoor thermal conditions without mechanical cooling systems.
