# Hardware Components

## 1. Building Structure

The house model is designed to demonstrate passive cooling techniques through building orientation, ventilation, shading, insulation, and thermal mass.

## 2. Thermal Insulation

Insulation is provided in selected wall and roof sections to reduce heat transfer from the outdoor environment to the indoor space.

## 3. Reflective Roof

A light-coloured or reflective roof surface reduces solar heat absorption and helps maintain a lower indoor temperature.

## 4. Natural Ventilation

Ventilation openings are incorporated to enable natural airflow and remove accumulated hot air from the building.

## 5. Windows and Shading

Windows provide ventilation and daylight, while overhangs and external shading reduce direct solar radiation entering the building.

## 6. Thermal Mass

Materials with high thermal mass can absorb heat during the day and delay its transfer to the indoor environment.

## 7. Temperature Monitoring

Temperature sensors can be installed at indoor and outdoor locations to compare temperature conditions and evaluate the cooling performance.

## 8. Optional Electronic Hardware

* ESP32 / Arduino
* DHT11 / DHT22 temperature and humidity sensor
* Jumper wires
* Breadboard
* USB power supply

## Working Principle

The system primarily uses passive methods rather than mechanical cooling. Solar heat gain is reduced using reflective surfaces and shading, while insulation limits heat transfer. Natural ventilation promotes air circulation and removes hot air. Thermal mass helps delay indoor temperature rise.

Temperature sensors can be used to monitor the indoor and outdoor conditions and evaluate the effectiveness of the passive cooling design.
